Platform Modules

End-to-end cyber-physical solutions — from sensor to cloud, from machine to decision.

Flagship Platform

MaintelliGenX

Unified orchestration layer — integrating RotorTwinX, AeroEngineTwinX, PowerPlantTwin, and RailTwin into a single physics-informed, data-driven operations intelligence platform. One dashboard. Every asset. Every domain.

RotorTwinX AeroEngineTwinX PowerPlantTwin RailTwin ProcessScheduler
  • Domain-selector landing: choose asset class, load its full twin module
  • Shared Health Index (HI) engine — monotonically decreasing, physics-grounded
  • Unified DOCX/PDF report generation across all modules
  • Validated on NASA C-MAPSS (709 engines · 159,359 cycles)
  • Streamlit · PyTorch · SimPy · Cloud VPS architecture

RotorTwinX

Rotating Machinery · VibLab IIT Kanpur

Digital twin for rotor-bearing fault classification. Raw vibration signals → STFT spectrograms → VibLab CNN v2 (custom architecture from IIT Kanpur) → t-SNE cluster visualisation → Health Index. Detects Unbalance, Crack, BPFO, BPFI with 93–100% accuracy.

  • STFT · wavelet signal processing pipeline
  • VibLab IIT Kanpur CNN v2 · LSTM · ResNet
  • t-SNE dimensionality visualisation
  • 93–100% accuracy on 5-class rotor dataset
  • Automated DOCX/PDF diagnostic report

AeroEngineTwinX

Turbofan Engine Prognostics

Physics-informed digital twin for gas turbine engine health management. Thermodynamic cycle reconstruction from partial sensor data yields component-resolved Health Index without run-to-failure labels. Validated on the NASA C-MAPSS benchmark across all four sub-datasets.

  • 4-step physics-informed HI pipeline
  • T-s / P-v thermodynamic cycle reconstruction
  • 87% component diagnosis accuracy (single-fault)
  • Fleet-wide monitoring dashboard
  • No run-to-failure labels required

PowerPlantTwin

Power Generation Asset Monitoring

Condition monitoring digital twin for power generation assets — turbines, generators, auxiliary machinery. Translates legacy LabVIEW PoP Monitor acquisitions into a Python-based analytics pipeline with FFT spectral analysis, harmonic identification, Health Index tracking, and anomaly detection.

  • LabVIEW → Python migration pathway
  • FFT · harmonic analysis · 1×, 2× tracking
  • Fault-frequency identification (bearing, shaft)
  • Health Index trending over operational life
  • Shares RotorTwinX vibration engine

Real-Time Monitoring

DAQ · Dashboard · HMI

Shopfloor data acquisition from heterogeneous machines — CNC, welding robots, manual work centres — homogenised and visualised in a live web dashboard. RFID-based material tracking and HMI integration, deployed and validated at MCF Raebareli. Remote access via cloud VPS.

  • Multi-machine DAQ homogenisation
  • RFID material & WIP tracking
  • Live web dashboard + historical analytics
  • Utilisation, cycle time, OEE metrics
  • Cloud VPS relay for remote monitoring

Process Scheduling

Shopfloor Digital Twin · SimPy Engine

Discrete-event simulation of the production shopfloor — each machine and component modelled as Python objects. Priority-based dispatching, automated Gantt chart generation, and bottleneck identification. Deployed at MCF Raebareli Shell Shop; reduced lead time for priority coaches by up to 10 days.

  • SimPy discrete-event at 1-minute resolution
  • Priority-based job dispatching rules
  • Gantt + machine utilisation analytics
  • Bottleneck detection (86–88% utilisation)
  • ERP integration flexibility

OT–IT Bridge

Operational · Information Technology

Platform bridging the physical shopfloor (Operational Technology — PLCs, CNCs, embedded systems, RFID, cameras) with enterprise analytics (Information Technology). Indigenous design ensures secure data ownership, with no dependence on proprietary vendor ecosystems.

  • Industrial controllers · embedded systems
  • Modbus · OPC-UA · MQTT · RS-232/485
  • Camera & scanner integration
  • Secure, indigenous data ownership
  • Scalable from single machine to factory

Major Projects

2019–present
Industry 4.0 · Railways

Technology Mission for Indian Railways (TMIR) — MCF Raebareli

Pilot-scale implementation of an integrated Industry 4.0 platform at Modern Coach Factory (MCF), Raebareli — India's most modern coach manufacturing facility. Three interconnected pillars: shopfloor monitoring, production scheduling, and RFID-based material tracking. Priority-based scheduling reduced completion time for highest-priority coach variants by up to 10 days vs random dispatching. Bottleneck analysis identified key stations at ~86–88% utilisation.

Digital Twin · Railways

Railway Digital Twin — Waltair Division, East Coast Railway

Design and implementation of a five-layer open-source digital twinning framework for Indian Railways, developed in collaboration with CRIS (Centre for Railway Information Systems). The Waltair (WAT) Division — serving Visakhapatnam Port, RINL steel, and petroleum freight — was selected as pilot. Framework spans data acquisition, model layer, analytics, and a cloud dashboard for remote monitoring. Presented at RAIL 26, Budapest.

Prof. Nalinaksh S. Vyas

Professor Emeritus, Department of Mechanical Engineering, IIT Kanpur
Former Chairman, Technology Mission for Indian Railways (TMIR), Govt. of India

Prof. Vyas holds a Ph.D. (1986), M.Tech. (1983) and B.Tech. (1980) — all in Mechanical Engineering — from IIT Delhi and IIT Bombay, and has been a faculty member at IIT Kanpur since 1987. His research spans Machine Dynamics, Nonlinear Parameter Estimation, Instrumentation, and AI/ML applications for Smart Infrastructure and Industry 4.0.

As Chairman of TMIR (till April 2022), he spearheaded the digital modernisation of one of the world's largest railway networks, including Industry 4.0 protocols at MCF Raebareli, on-board diagnostics for rolling stock, and deep learning for rail asset management.

He has held leadership roles including Head of Mechanical Engineering, Nuclear Engineering, and Centre for Mechatronics at IIT Kanpur; Vice Chancellor of Rajasthan Technical University (2013–15); and Visiting Professor at Virginia Tech, INSA Lyon, Lulea University, and National Chung Cheng University. He is a Founding Director of the International Society on Asset Management, Australia.
